Upload & name

Everything here runs in your browser — your files never leave your computer. Filenames are P+ — P+ overrides base Brawl wherever the two differ.

Drop your modded textures here PNG, JPG, BMP, WebP — or a Brawl/P+ .pac / .pcs file: the textures inside are extracted and named automatically (it skips tiny placeholder textures under 8px, the rare C14X2 format, stops at 4000 textures per file, and auto-skips textures the data says are the same file for every costume — one click un-skips those if your mod changes one). Everything comes back renamed for Dolphin in one zip, ready for the custom-textures folder. Anything the page isn't sure about waits for your review instead of being renamed blindly.
Set every included texture to a slot

unzip it into Dolphin's User\Load\Textures\ folder, then turn on Graphics → Advanced → Load Custom Textures. Files that still need a name are left out of the zip until you pick one.

Built from a Brawl disc and Project+ 3.2.1. Names come from the textures' own data (Dolphin hashes them), so two costumes showing the same filename means the texture is genuinely identical.
